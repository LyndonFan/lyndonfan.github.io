I watched a [Dog Day Evening (一個部門的誕生)](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%80%E5%80%8B%E9%83%A8%E9%96%80%E7%9A%84%E8%AA%95%E7%94%9F#%E8%A3%BD%E4%BD%9C) (no not dog day afternoon) a while ago. It's quite funny and decent!

## Spoiler free review

So our main character Ducky* kidnaps hostages in a TV station to cancel his TV subscription. But bureaucracy is just starting...
(Yes this is in the trailer)

The overall premise is wild, and the movie runs with it -- with a few bumps. Each character gets their moment, and you empathise them. By the end you also want to cancel your subscription too.

The premise was absurd, but it just kept going! Each time it made sense as he "released" more hostages.

Adding the person who plays the mascot provided more background, and the news reporter for linked us to the outside world. Limiting to just the reception area made things more tangible.

Oh and the gun (like Chekhov’s). The second time it fired was too dramatic since it was to end the movie, though you can only maintain suspense so much.

The main awkward bit was when Ducky was comforting his grandma by saying this was all just a movie. The tension dropped with the fourth wall break, and everyone acted normally? I think the joke overstayed its welcome.

The bureaucracy and how the TV station is serving its own needs is ... familiar. You'd appreciate more if you lived in Hong Kong during the time when it was made/written. The end scene gets you.

Also this was inspired by [a real event](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%80%E5%80%8B%E9%83%A8%E9%96%80%E7%9A%84%E8%AA%95%E7%94%9F#%E8%A3%BD%E4%BD%9C) -- I don't quite remember it myself!

Would recommend to anyone who has lingering subscriptions and is trapped by dark ~~magic~~ patterns
