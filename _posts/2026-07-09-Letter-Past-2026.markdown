Dear Lyndon,

Thanks again for writing. I'm out of the country as I write this, so my thoughts might be a bit scattered.

## How's your week?

Pretty good! Travelling in Europe for fun -- you might have a guess why.

## Anything you've done exciting recently?

Aside from the above, the past 3 months were quite busy between moving and travelling. If anything, it was a bit too much for me!

## What's your typical schedule?

Work is more planned and regular, and there's a fixed list of after-work activites. From coworkers, friends, and myself too!

## How's your career?

Pretty good, I think? You'll finish the project on hand (eventually :upsidedownsmile:) but learn some lessons on the way. Then you'll get to work on something more your forte.

Outside of work, (sigh), not much. My weekly activities occupy a good bunch of my time, and the rest I'm not using them as well as I want.

## What about your support network?

It hasn't expanded much, but grown stronger over the past year -- I guess living together for >1 year helps. You'll also talk to and maybe confide with more people so it helps.

## How _am_ I?

Tired. A bit physically (go to sleep you dummy), but also mentally? The past few months felt like I'm juggling many things at once between career, interests, and sorting out what to get for dinner. Especially when my brain feels fried after work.

Sometimes I feel stuck or even falling behind, and end up twiddling my fingers. But compiling the below list forced me to look back on what things I've done over the past year, and it's more than I thought!

### (sigh) AI

(Obligatory AI mention because it's 2026)

If you think AI is prevalent in 2025, oh boy have I got news for you.

It's still front and center today, with some wild and unpredictable news about the models, people, and companies. Besides those, there's still a mix of concerns:

- how useful AI is, e.g. full automation, helpful but needs work, to just noise
- what environmental and material costs it has, if any
- how it affects education / learning
- how it affects economy / politics / the general world (despite my limited knowledge of these fields)

In some regards I've updated, mostly because the models are improving (this is almost certainly going to happen even for you, so I'm not counting this as a spoiler) and there are, erm, other developments.

Just talk to me and you'll get a better picture.
