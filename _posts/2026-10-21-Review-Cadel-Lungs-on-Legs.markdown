I watched [Cadel: Lungs on Legs](https://www.cadeltheplay.com/) recently. It was quite good!

## What's this?

It's a 1 hour play about Cadel Evans, an Australian cyclist who went on to win the Tour de France. It's a solo show performed by Connor Delves re-enacting Cadel's story, while he's on a bike for the whole hour.

## Spoiler-free Review

It's more exciting than it sounds! He's on the bike for a good chunk of the time, though not literally the whole hour (I was got by the marketing). It's a relatively straightforward story, but the bike and setup make you feel the story and his emotions.

I came in knowing ~nothing about Cadel / Tour de France, but still managed to follow the plot well.
The performance started with Connor pedalling, but after a bit he stepping and started a monologue. His performance was a bit camp, especially for showing Cadel's childhood / younger years. But I liked how he made use of the whole bike, looking through the wheels like a kid at a display cabinet.
<!-- He also says "lungs on legs", a nickname he earned at the sports academy / training. Roll credits! (CinemaSins ping) -->

When he starts racing, the displays help supplement the vibe. There are shots showing the route, rankings, and even race commentators. He also changes his jersey and bike over the races. At each race, he embodies and shows Cadel's emotions. When in 2009 he lost his lead because the replacement bike arrived late and he had to use a wrong water bottle, he was crying, almost whining. I _felt_ his frustration and pent up anger with existing team.

Finally, after years of trying, he finally wins Tour de France in 2011! It feels earned after all his misfortunes and trials. His re-enaction of the speech with posture was pretty good -- there was a clip of Cadel on the podium playing on screen.

Overall, pretty good! Would recommend it, especially if you're into cycling and/or sports.
